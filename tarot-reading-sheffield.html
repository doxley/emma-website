A reading that takes its time

Emma reads with the full 78-card deck and doesn't rush. She keeps turning cards until the picture is clear, so you leave with real depth rather than a few quick answers. Bring a question about relationships, work, family or a decision you're facing, or simply let the cards lead.

Emma sees tarot as a tool for reflection and insight rather than fixed predictions: a way to look at your situation from a new angle, recognise patterns and feel clearer about your next step. Many clients find her readings especially supportive through grief, change and new beginnings.

What happens in a tarot reading

  1. A short chat. Tell Emma as much or as little as you like about what's on your mind.
  2. The cards. You shuffle (or Emma shuffles for you online), and she lays out a spread suited to your question.
  3. The reading. Emma talks you through what she sees, card by card, and you're welcome to ask questions as you go.
  4. Clarity. She'll keep drawing cards until the picture feels complete.

Want to read the cards yourself? Emma also runs one-to-one tarot courses in Sheffield.

Tarot readings are for guidance and reflection. They aren't a substitute for medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

Questions people ask

Do I need a question for a tarot reading?

No. Some people come with a specific question; others prefer a general reading and let the cards show what's important right now. Both work well.

Is an online tarot reading as good as one in person?

Yes. Emma gives online readings with the same deck, the same time and the same depth. Many people like being able to relax at home.

How does an online tarot reading work?

Book a Tuesday slot online and pay securely. At your appointment time, Emma calls you by video on Facebook Messenger. Make sure you have Messenger installed and can find a quiet spot.

Can tarot predict the future?

Emma sees tarot as a tool for insight and reflection rather than fixed predictions. The cards can help you see your situation more clearly and think about the choices in front of you.
