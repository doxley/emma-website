Tarot has a fascinating history that stretches back to 15th-century Europe, where the cards were originally created as a game before gradually evolving into a powerful tool for spiritual exploration, reflection and self-discovery. Over the centuries, tarot has captivated people with its rich symbolism, archetypal imagery and timeless wisdom, offering a unique way to explore the mysteries of life and the human experience. Each card tells a story, holding layers of meaning that invite us to look beyond the surface and discover new perspectives on ourselves, our relationships and the world around us.

Tarot is so much more than a fortune-telling tool; it is a beautiful pathway to self-understanding, confidence and personal empowerment. By learning to work with the cards, you can begin to trust your intuition, quieten self-doubt and gain clarity when navigating life's challenges and opportunities. Tarot encourages you to pause, reflect and listen to your inner wisdom, helping you recognise patterns, uncover hidden strengths and reconnect with the person you truly are. Rather than simply predicting what might happen, tarot opens up a conversation with yourself, empowering you to make conscious choices and approach your future with greater awareness, courage and confidence.

I invite you to join my tarot course and discover the magic, depth and beauty of this extraordinary practice for yourself. Whether you are completely new to tarot or feel drawn to deepen your existing knowledge, this is an opportunity to explore the cards in a supportive and inspiring way, develop your intuition and discover the many ways tarot can enrich your life. Together, we will unlock the stories within the cards and explore the insights they have to offer, one step at a time. There is a whole world of wisdom waiting to be discovered, and you never know where the journey may lead.

Come with an open heart and a curious mind, and let tarot take you on a beautiful, unexpected journey of self-discovery, empowerment and possibility.
— Emma
